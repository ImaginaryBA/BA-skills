# Stakeholders are always told when AI processes their input

Every interview guide and questionnaire opens with a notice that the session's notes or answers will be processed with the company-approved BA-skills library, linking to its glossary, and that the BA reviews every output. Disclosure is mandatory and no skill may omit it, because transparency towards the people whose words we process is a company principle and may also be a data-protection requirement. The wording, set by the library owner, lives in `shared/ai-use.md`.
