# Stakeholders are always told when AI processes their input

Every interview guide and questionnaire opens with a notice that the session's notes or answers will be processed with an approved AI tool, that names are replaced by roles, and that the BA reviews every output. Disclosure is mandatory and no skill may omit it, because transparency towards the people whose words we process is a company principle and may also be a data-protection requirement. The default wording lives in `shared/ai-use.md` and is replaced by company wording when one exists.
