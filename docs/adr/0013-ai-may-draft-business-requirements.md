# Skills may draft business requirements, always flagged for the BA

`findings-to-requirements` may draft business requirements from the business need in `INITIATIVE.md` and the findings, so the trace from stakeholder and solution requirements to business requirements is never left empty. Because business requirements carry the sponsor's intent, every drafted BR is labelled AI-generated and listed for the BA's attention, and only the BA (with the sponsor) confirms them. Anything without a traceable source becomes an open question rather than a requirement.
