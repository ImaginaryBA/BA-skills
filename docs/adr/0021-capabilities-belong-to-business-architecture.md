# Capabilities belong to the business architecture team

BABOK's current-state analysis covers business capabilities, but in this company the business architecture team owns the capability maps. So no skill drafts, infers or edits a capability or a capability map. An analysis may name capabilities only as the team's capability map names them, citing the map as a supplied document. A capability the analysis needs but the map lacks becomes an open question for the business architecture team, never a new capability. `assess-current-state` therefore covers processes, not capabilities, in its own sections.
